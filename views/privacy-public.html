Privacy & data

Version 21 October 2026

Spearpoint AI

Spearpoint Search operates this employer search planning tool. Please do not submit CVs, candidate names or sensitive personal information. Use only hiring details you are authorised to share.

Employer briefing requests

With your consent, Spearpoint stores the name, email, company and hiring requirements you submit so a consultant can respond to your brief. Submissions are stored privately in the configured Supabase project. Brief records expire after 30 days and are removed by a daily retention job. Access is limited to authorised operators.

Hiring market briefs

The downloadable brief uses your selected role, market, niche and function. Downloading requires no email address and contains no candidate identities. Requesting a tailored follow-up uses the employer briefing form and its consent, access and 30-day retention controls. It records a request for a consultant; it does not automatically send an email or subscribe you to marketing. The salary guide opens on the Spearpoint website under its own privacy terms.

Private candidate search and model CVs

The site may search Recruit CRM privately on the server using the selected role. Names, employers, contact details, CVs and private career histories are not sent to the browser. Public model CVs are built from curated role templates and the selected brief. They do not describe an individual candidate, a completed shortlist or verified availability. A consultant handles validation and any subsequent sharing or introduction with the appropriate permissions.

Service providers and booking

Hosting uses Vercel and brief storage uses Supabase when configured. Searches may use Recruit CRM through a private server. The booking link opens HubSpot, where its own privacy terms apply. Automatic HubSpot contact creation is disabled by default and may only be enabled after operator authorisation. No advertising or analytics trackers are included.

Security and retention

For abuse prevention, an hourly rate-limit record uses a keyed hash of the request IP address, rather than storing the raw address in our application tables. Expired records are deleted daily. Hosting and provider infrastructure may maintain separate operational logs under their own retention policies. The public tool does not require an account. Private operator testing uses a separate authenticated staging address.

Requests and corrections

For access, deletion, correction or consent withdrawal, contact sam@spearpointsearch.com. Include your request reference if available. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out. See also the Spearpoint Search privacy policy.
