Privacy & data

Staging notice • Version 21 October 2026

A private testing environment

This preview is for authorised testers. Use test employer details. Do not enter CVs, sensitive personal data, candidate names, or confidential hiring information.

Employer briefing requests

With your consent, Spearpoint stores the name, email, company and hiring requirements you submit so a consultant can respond to your brief. Submissions are stored privately in the configured Supabase project. Brief records expire after 30 days and are removed by a daily retention job. Access is limited to authorised operators.

Hiring market briefs

The downloadable brief uses your selected role, market, niche and function. Downloading requires no email address and contains no candidate identities. Requesting a tailored follow-up uses the employer briefing form and its consent, access and 30-day retention controls. It records a request for a consultant; it does not automatically send an email or subscribe you to marketing. The salary guide opens on the Spearpoint website under its own privacy terms.

Private candidate search and search previews

The site may search Recruit CRM privately on the server using the selected role. Names, employers, contact details, CVs and private career histories are not sent to the browser. Public search previews are built from curated role templates and the selected brief. They do not describe an individual candidate, a completed shortlist or verified availability. A consultant handles validation and any subsequent sharing or introduction with the appropriate permissions.

Service providers and booking

Hosting uses Vercel and brief storage uses Supabase when configured. Searches may use Recruit CRM through a private server. The booking link opens HubSpot, where its own privacy terms apply. Automatic HubSpot contact creation is disabled by default and may only be enabled after operator authorisation. No advertising or analytics trackers are included.

Security and retention

For abuse prevention, an hourly rate-limit record uses a keyed hash of the request IP address, rather than storing the raw address in our application tables. Expired records are deleted daily. Hosting and provider infrastructure may maintain separate operational logs under their own retention policies. Browser Basic Authentication credentials remain in the browser session; close the private browsing window after testing.

Requests and corrections

For access, deletion, correction or consent withdrawal during this private trial, contact the Spearpoint operator who gave you staging access. This notice must be updated with the legal controller, a direct privacy contact and applicable processing details before any public launch.

AI-assisted search previews

If you supply a custom role or job requirements, we may send those requirements through Vercel AI Gateway to Google Gemini to create a fictional example CV. Do not include personal details, employer secrets or confidential documents. We remove common email, phone and URL patterns before generation; this does not guarantee anonymisation. Recruit CRM candidate records are not sent to the AI service. Generated CVs and a keyed request fingerprint are retained privately for up to 30 days for reuse; the pasted description itself is not saved in this cache. Example career histories and figures are hypothetical and do not prove a candidate match, availability or interest.
