Privacy & data

Staging notice • Version 30 October 2026

A private testing environment

This preview is for authorised testers. Use test employer details. Do not enter CVs, sensitive personal data, candidate names, or confidential hiring information.

Employer briefing requests

With your consent, Spearpoint stores the name, email, company and hiring requirements you submit so a consultant can respond to your brief. Submissions are stored privately in the configured Supabase project. Brief records expire after 30 days and are removed by a daily retention job. Access is limited to authorised operators.

Hiring market briefs

The downloadable brief uses your selected role, market, niche and function. Downloading requires no email address and contains no candidate identities. Requesting a tailored follow-up uses the employer briefing form and its consent, access and 30-day retention controls. It records a request for a consultant; it does not automatically send an email or subscribe you to marketing. The salary guide opens on the Spearpoint website under its own privacy terms.

Confidential candidate search

Recruit CRM is searched privately on the server. Only manually approved, anonymised profile text is returned when current recorded approval for external display, a review within 30 days and the structured search requirements are satisfied. The approval record distinguishes candidate permission from owner approval of an anonymised professional summary. Owner approval does not establish candidate consent or permission for an introduction. Names, exact employers, contact details, CVs, private notes and CRM identifiers are not returned. Results have an opaque reference so a specialist can identify the requested profile internally. Current interest, availability and permission for an introduction are checked separately.

Service providers and booking

Hosting uses Vercel and brief storage uses Supabase when configured. Searches may use Recruit CRM through a private server. The booking link opens HubSpot, where its own privacy terms apply. Automatic HubSpot contact creation is disabled by default and may only be enabled after operator authorisation. No advertising or analytics trackers are included.

Security and retention

For abuse prevention, an hourly rate-limit record uses a keyed hash of the request IP address, rather than storing the raw address in our application tables. Expired records are deleted daily. Hosting and provider infrastructure may maintain separate operational logs under their own retention policies. Browser Basic Authentication credentials remain in the browser session; close the private browsing window after testing.

Requests and corrections

For access, deletion, correction or consent withdrawal during this private trial, contact the Spearpoint operator who gave you staging access. This notice must be updated with the legal controller, a direct privacy contact and applicable processing details before any public launch.

Search requirements and AI interpretation

Guided search uses role, sector, location and essential skills against approved information. Additional brief notes remain for consultant review. Natural-language mandates are sent through Vercel AI Gateway to Google Gemini to extract structured requirements. Do not enter candidate names, contact details, sensitive company information or other confidential personal data in a mandate. Candidate records and summaries are not sent to the model. Interpretation must be reviewed before searching. Matching uses reviewed role groups, explicit sector tags and evidence categories, not AI-generated suitability scores. Unmapped requirements and missing evidence are shown as gaps. Reviewed summaries are not independently verified and do not establish current availability.

Your private workspace

Workspace accounts use Supabase Auth, verified email and a password. Access and refresh tokens are held in an encrypted, secure HttpOnly cookie for up to seven days. The browser also holds selected opaque profile references in session storage while preparing a shortlist. Saved mandates, shortlists and manually recorded outcomes are accessible to their account owner and available for up to 180 days. After expiry they become inaccessible through the workspace. Automatic physical deletion of workspace records is awaiting operator approval; contact us to request deletion in the meantime. You can delete saved searches and shortlists in the workspace. Outcome entries may remain in aggregate reporting for the rest of their retention period. Contact us to delete your account or correct outcome records.

Shared shortlists and outcome reporting

A shared shortlist uses a hard-to-guess link. Anyone with that link can view its title and currently approved anonymised summaries until it expires after seven days or is revoked. Creating a replacement link revokes the old one. Share links contain no raw CRM identifiers. Share only with the intended hiring team. Introduction requests and acceptances, shortlist acceptances, estimated time saved and staff delivery costs are recorded by the workspace owner; the service does not independently verify these entries or send candidate messages. Search activity is recorded for signed-in users to measure repeat usage. AI billing costs are shown as unreconciled rather than invented.

Employer role workspaces and public research

Your employer workspace stores company context, role descriptions, compensation notes, approved search criteria, candidate pipeline feedback and introduction requests. These records are private to the account owner. AI brief drafting sends the role description and compensation you submit to the interpretation provider. Optional company research sends only the approved company name and website to Perplexity through Vercel AI Gateway. Optional public candidate research sends only the approved role, sectors, location and selected professional capabilities; it does not send private company context, the full job description, compensation, feedback or CRM records.

Public research can discover publicly indexed professional profiles, including LinkedIn pages where available to the search provider. It does not use a direct LinkedIn member-database connection. Public profile names, reported employers, source URLs and source excerpts are stored in a restricted specialist review queue. Employer comparison views show only a pending-review reference until a verified specialist approves an anonymised summary. Public information and AI research are not independent verification of experience, availability or permission for contact.

New public research records expire from application access after 30 days; approved public summaries require review at least every 30 days. Role, company, pipeline and introduction records expire from application access after 180 days. Physical deletion for new workspace records awaits operator approval of retention cleanup. Introduction requests do not automatically send messages or release candidate identity. Specialist confirmation of candidate permission is a separate step, and marking an introduction completed records an event rather than sending an email. Access and correction requests can be made to sam@spearpointsearch.com.
