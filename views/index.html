4.1 / Before you brief us

Clear terms for a confidential search.

Free exploration is the starting point. A senior appointment needs a specific mandate and a trusted conversation.

Who is Spearpoint AI built for?+

Employers planning senior specialist and leadership hires in capital markets and market infrastructure, with target annual base salaries from US$150,000. The focus includes exchanges, market makers, prop trading firms, hedge funds, digital assets, financial AI infrastructure, fintech and payments.

What is free, and what is paid?+

The guided exploration, profile preview and downloadable hiring market brief are free. A retained search is a separate, consultant-led engagement. We agree the mandate, scope and fees with you before that work begins.

Can we try it before committing to a search?+

Yes. Explore a role and its target profile without a commitment. The preview is a role-based search preview. It helps you define the right experience and capabilities before discussing a confidential search.

Why are names and CVs kept private?+

The preview is a search preview and contains no individual candidate details. Our CRM records stay private. Senior talent may not be actively looking, so any later sharing of identifying details or a CV needs the appropriate candidate permission and an agreed purpose.

Can we contact a candidate directly?+

Start with a briefing. We validate the role and the candidate’s fit, interest and willingness to be introduced. A briefing does not guarantee a CV, an introduction or candidate availability.

Why does a senior search need human involvement?+

A senior appointment depends on mandate, judgement, timing and trust. We use a specific conversation to understand the business, then approach relevant people in the context of that role. Automated, generic outreach cannot establish those conditions.

How does Spearpoint find relevant candidates?+

We search our database of 50,000+ capital markets candidates to identify relevant talent. A consultant then confirms fit, availability and interest before any introduction.

Does the salary guide guarantee an offer will be accepted?+

No. The guide is a reference point. Scope, location, seniority, incentives and the candidate’s circumstances all matter. Use a briefing to validate the package for your specific mandate.

What happens after we book a briefing?+

We discuss the business, the first-year outcomes, the package and the search constraints. We then recommend the next step. Candidate approaches and any retained search engagement follow an agreed process, with permissions and scope confirmed.
